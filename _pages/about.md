 I'm a phytoplankton ecologist and biological oceanographer broadly interested in the connections between organism physiology and behavior, aquatic ecosystem function, human health, and environmental change. I'm currently a postdoctoral investigator at the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, working in the [Alexander Lab](https://alexanderlabwhoi.github.io) to try to understand dinoflagellate population dynamics. More about my research [here](research.md). 


When not wrangling dinoflagellates in salt marshes, doing experiments in the lab, or queuing up computing jobs on our HPC, I enjoy running and cycling (when the weather is good) as well as doing pottery, knitting, or sewing (when the weather is bad).
